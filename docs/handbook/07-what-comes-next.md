# What Comes Next

*The road ahead — and the words you'll need.*

## The roadmap

The platform is complete at its core: publish, debate, curate, moderate, discover. The frontier is depth, not breadth:

- **The Arena**: full challenge ladders — rebuttal rankings with comparison views and community verdicts. The bones exist (counter-lists); the spectacle layer is next.
- **Richer discovery**: category tree management in the admin UI, personalized surfaces, Hall of Fame curation.
- **Engagement**: email notifications, design themes, post revision histories.
- **Scale**: everything above runs on today's single-server footprint well into six-figure monthly traffic; horizontal scaling is a known, unstarted project.

Nothing on this list is load-bearing. If development paused tomorrow, the platform as it stands runs, earns attention, and compounds its catalog indefinitely.

## Glossary

- **Top List** — a ranked list of ten with written defenses. The atomic unit.
- **Counter List** — a rival ranking challenging an existing one.
- **Fact Drop** — a short sourced statement.
- **Trust tier** — an identity's standing (unknown through scholar), earned by behavior.
- **Double-blind review** — moderation where the reviewer never sees the author's standing.
- **Seed phrase** — twelve words that restore an identity on a new device.
- **Proof-of-effort** — a small computational puzzle that makes mass fake identities expensive.
- **Sitemap / IndexNow** — the machinery that tells search engines about new content within minutes.
