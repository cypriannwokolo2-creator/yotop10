# Inside the Arena

*A tour of what people actually do here.*

The platform serves three kinds of visitor: the **reader** who browses and votes, the **contributor** who submits, and the **moderator** who keeps the stage clean. This chapter walks the first two journeys.

## The eight things you can publish

| Format | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Top List | Ten ranked items, each with a written defense | Top 10 Most Influential Scientists Ever |
| This vs That | Two-item duel with live side voting | iPhone vs Samsung — which is better? |
| Who Is Better | Multi-candidate verdict | Messi, Ronaldo, Pelé — Final Verdict |
| Best / Worst Of | Time-scoped or inverted lists | Best Movies of 2024 |
| Hidden Gems | Underrated topics, ten at a time | 10 Countries No One Talks About |
| Counter List | A direct rebuttal to someone else's ranking | My rebuttal to your Top 10 Rappers |
| Fact Drop | One short, sourced statement | Honey never spoils |
| Article | Long-form deep dive with cover art and citations | Any full-length explainer |

## A reader's visit

The homepage opens on trending lists, debates, and facts. Opening a list shows the ranking, each item's justification, and the live vote splits. On a debate, one tap picks a side and the percentages move in real time. On any item, a reader can fire a counter-list — a rival ranking linked to the original, so challenges never get lost. Argument threads under items carry rebuttals weighted by community fire. Everything is readable without ever identifying yourself.

## A contributor's sixty seconds

Tap submit, pick a format, add items with a line of defense each, attach images and sources, choose from 341 categories, and publish. Drafts autosave. The submission enters a human review queue; on approval it goes live and the author is notified. No account creation, no email confirmation, no onboarding funnel — the funnel *is* the content.

## One list, end to end

Take a real example: *Best 3 Chocolate Cakes of 2026*. Three items — an Argentine no-bake caramel cake, an Italian flourless classic, a Swedish sticky perfection — each with a paragraph saying *why it belongs*. A reader from Buenos Aires disagrees with the order and fires a counter-list putting the Argentine first with its own defense. Voters split. The original author replies in the argument thread. One page has now become a small festival: the list, its challenger, the votes, the rebuttals — all linked, all ranked, all permanent. Multiply that dynamic by every topic people care about and you have the catalog.

## For the content manager

Your daily surface is the review queue: pending lists and articles with approve/reject/edit actions, bulk operations, category management, user trust controls, audit logs, and platform statistics. Edits require a stated reason, and the author is notified with that reason automatically. Nothing publishes without passing through you or your moderators.
