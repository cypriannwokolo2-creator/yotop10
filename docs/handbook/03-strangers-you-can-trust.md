# Strangers You Can Trust

*Identity without passwords, quality without signup walls.*

This is the chapter investors usually read twice, because it contains the platform's structural bet.

## The problem with accounts

Accounts were designed to solve trust: know who someone is, and you can judge what they say. But accounts cost the platform its most precious resource — the casual visitor. Every required field sheds contributors. And every stored email address is a breach waiting to happen and a privacy policy waiting to be violated.

YoTop10 inverts the model. **There are no accounts in the traditional sense.** A visitor is recognized by their device. First visit mints an anonymous identity automatically; the platform watches behavior, not identity documents. Contributors build standing under stable pseudonyms. If they lose their device, an optional seed phrase (twelve ordinary words, like a crypto wallet) restores them. No passwords exist anywhere in the system — there is literally nothing to phish, stuff, or reset.

## Trust tiers

Behavior earns standing. New identities start as unknowns; consistent, approved contributions climb through tiers toward scholar; abuse sinks toward troll. The tier governs everything: how often you may post, whether your submissions skip parts of the queue, how much weight your votes carry. Automated abuse — bot floods, handle squatting, scripted voting — is met with escalating friction: computational puzzles that cost real seconds per fake identity, per-IP rate limits, and maturity locks that freeze sensitive actions for young accounts.

## Human review

Automation screens; humans decide. Every submission lands in a moderation queue where named moderators with granular, audited permissions approve, reject (with a stated reason the author sees), or request revision. Approval and rejection both notify the author. Every moderation action writes to an immutable audit log — who did what, to which content, when.

## For the content manager

Your trust tools: review queues for posts and articles, per-user trust scores and restriction controls, rate-limit tuning, moderator accounts with 31 granular permissions across four preset roles, and full audit trails. The system is designed so that one careful person can hold the line for a community of thousands.

## For the investor

This is the moat. A platform with no signup wall *and* no spam problem is something the industry has largely failed to build. The trust machinery — device identity, tiered rate limits, proof-of-effort gates, double-blind human review — is the most engineered part of YoTop10, and the hardest to replicate.
